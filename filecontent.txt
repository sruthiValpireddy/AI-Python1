
Itinerary: Hyderabad -> Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram)

Overview:
- Distance: ~1,200 km by road; ~1.5 hr flight
- Best options: Flight (fastest), Train (overnight), Bus/car (scenic, longer)

Sample 3-day plan

Day 0 - Travel
- Morning: Flight from Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (HYD) to Trivandrum International Airport (TRV). Typical duration: 1h 45m.
- Alternative: Overnight train from Hyderabad Decan/Hyderabad to Trivandrum Central (TLH) — book sleeper/AC as preferred.
- Arrival: Transfer to hotel, relax and local walk in the evening (MG Road or nearby beach).

Day 1 - City & Culture
- Morning: Visit Padmanabhaswamy Temple (dress code applies) and nearby East Fort area.
- Late morning: Napier Museum and Sri Chitra Art Gallery.
- Afternoon: Lunch with Kerala cuisine (try appam, fish curry).
- Evening: Kovalam Beach (sunset) — light dinner at a beachside cafe.

Day 2 - Nearby Attractions
- Morning: Visit Veli Tourist Village (boat ride on backwaters) or Poovar Island backwater cruise.
- Midday: Lunch and return to city.
- Afternoon: Explore local markets (Chalai Market) and shop for spices, handicrafts.
- Evening: Attend a Kathakali or classical performance if available.
Optional Extensions
- Kanyakumari (3–4 hr drive) for sunrise views
- Alleppey backwaters (train/drive) for overnight houseboat experience
